Spike (b): AI-cursor overlay (NSPanel via tauri-nspanel 2.1.1, Tauri 2.12.5)
Run 14 Nov 2026 on the Mac mini (macOS 26.5, one display "Display #5409", 1920x1080 pt, scale 1).
Harness: launch spikes/test-target (our own window) with --activate, then
  target/debug/deskhand --demo --exit-after 40 [--capturable]
under a clean env, take /usr/sbin/screencapture frames every ~0.3 s, find the
cursor's pink (#ff2d95) pixels, then call the MCP click tool on the test button
through the overlay (spikes/mcp-call.py screenshot + click).
